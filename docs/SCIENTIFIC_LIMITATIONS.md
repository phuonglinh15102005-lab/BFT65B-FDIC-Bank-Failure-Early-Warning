# Scientific limitations, separate from implementation defects

- **Historical filing/revision vintages:** audited quarter-lag alignment on a contemporary FDIC snapshot does not reconstruct each historical publication or revision. Additional code cannot supply unobserved vintages.
- **Costs:** FDIC resolution COST is observed ex post. Supervisory review fees and avoidable-loss fractions are assumptions. The primary ratio r=20, the 5%-of-median review-fee proxy and alternative scenarios are not measured causal savings. The frozen threshold is conditionally optimal for the specified development OOF proxy and alarm units, not a globally optimal intervention.
- **Evaluation units:** 326/341 detected events (95.60%) and approximately 40.98% COST-weighted recall use different denominators. Neither measures a causal avoided-loss rate.
- **Bootstrap provenance:** independently verified posthoc test/development intervals do not recover the unlocated historical producer, seed or draw definition. Conditional selected-OOF uncertainty is neither selection-adjusted nor a macro-block bootstrap.
- **Late ablation:** three additional development models were completed on 18 November 2026, after the original final-test lock. This is a late robustness analysis. Preserve mixed fold outcomes and the small mean AP difference; do not describe it as a pre-lock experiment or use it to revise the frozen model.
- **Reproduction and submission:** source verification, prior aggregate verification and checkpoint replay have distinct scopes. Fresh acquisition → original grids/refit → final scoring is NOT EXECUTED under the current restrictions. Public repository access and the final academic submission remain separately unverified.

This release repairs implementation issues in dispatch, resource discovery, output isolation, offline cache selection and numerical comparison. These repairs do not establish that the original frozen scientific outputs were incorrect.
