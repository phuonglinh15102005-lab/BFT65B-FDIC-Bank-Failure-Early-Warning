## Suggested pilot gates (prospective, not validated or statutory)
1. All scored cases have traceable source/version hashes; zero unauthorized future versions or unresolved duplicate keys. Missing/unusable filings explicitly logged and not silently dropped. Target 100% score repeatability within prespecified tolerance.
2. All matured cases independently adjudicated or marked unresolved; never silently code incomplete observation as negative. Four-quarter horizon; initial 8-quarter scoring + 4-quarter maturation plan.
3. All ordinary review allocations, deferrals, overrides and urgent escalations logged; zero unrecorded queue losses. Agree capacity and review SLA before pilot, measure staff hours, p95 wait and overdue backlog; urgent legal escalation bypasses ordinary cap.
4. Compare model against transparent local baseline at equal capacity using distinct-event recall, severity coverage and workload; require no unresolved severe omission without independent review. Illustrative precision screen: 25 distinct matured events and observed recall >=80%, with Wilson 95% lower bound >=60% (20/25 gives 60.87–91.14%, under independent-binomial approximation). Worst-case Wilson half-width at n=25 is 18.25 percentage points. This is a rough precision example, not a statistical guarantee under correlated systemic events, not required by the assignment, and may be unattainable in a small local population. No automatic deployment; validate by regime and extend shadow mode if insufficient evidence. Never count repeated positive bank-quarters as independent events.

The proposed criteria should be pre-agreed by supervisory owners; no real Vietnam event data, hours, staffing costs, SLA or adoption results are available. Do not credit proposed report edits as implemented.
